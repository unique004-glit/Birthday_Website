Happy Birthday Queen Mother

To our dearest Mummy

Today, we celebrate the extraordinary woman whose love has been our shelter, whose prayers have carried us, and whose strength continues to inspire us every day.

Thank you, Momma, for every sacrifice, every word of wisdom, every warm embrace, and every quiet act of love. Your care has shaped our lives in ways words can never fully express.

May this new year bring you overflowing joy, sound health, peace of mind, favour, and every beautiful blessing your heart desires. You deserve to be celebrated today and always.

With all our love, always.
